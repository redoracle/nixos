# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to maintain a technically rigorous, welcoming, and respectful open-source community. Everyone participating in this project is expected to treat others professionally, regardless of experience, background, identity, or perspective.

## Expected behavior

- Give constructive, actionable feedback; critique code and ideas rather than people.
- Assume good intent, communicate clearly, and acknowledge mistakes.
- Respect others' time, privacy, and security.
- Help newcomers understand the project's technical and safety requirements.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

These standards apply to this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, moderate discussions, or restrict participation when needed. Reports will be considered with discretion and without public disclosure of sensitive information.

For GitHub-hosted abuse, use [GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-abuse-and-harassment-policy). For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and avoid public exploit details.

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
